# 1. ASAA — Organization and Ecosystem

## Identity and purpose
ASAA is the umbrella organization and technology ecosystem for its products, internal capabilities, and future approved client work. Its GitHub organization is [ASAA1229](https://github.com/ASAA1229). The [organization repository](https://github.com/ASAA1229/organization) is the central place for organizational descriptions, policies, decisions, project summaries, risks, and reports.

ASAA aims to build, govern, operate, and improve useful software products and technology services. The intended model combines consistent governance, reusable engineering, AI-assisted development with safeguards, clear product ownership, professional delivery, cost awareness, security, and privacy.

## Vision and mission
**Vision:** Build an adaptable technology organization where projects can be planned, delivered, monitored, reused, secured, and improved through consistent governance without forcing every product into one architecture or release cycle.

**Mission:** Deliver reliable software and digital experiences; reduce repeated effort through reusable assets; use AI and automation responsibly; make requirements, decisions, risks, status, and evidence visible; and separate planning, implementation, testing, acceptance, and release.

### How they fit together
- **ASAA:** organization visibility, governance, project/repository understanding, requirements, evidence, and reporting. The boundary for HR, payroll, finance, procurement, vendor, and customer operations is not yet settled.
- **Portfolio:** profile-specific public presentation and content management; it is not the organization governance system.
- **JARVIS:** personal AI, voice, developer assistance, and automation; it remains a separate product with privacy and action-safety constraints.
- **Templates:** reusable development starting points and engineering assets; it does not own other projects' source code or releases by default.

Shared security, branch, traceability, evidence, and cost standards do not imply shared databases, deployments, credentials, or unrestricted data access.

## Project classification
- **Personal:** only when the owner explicitly labels the project personal. These projects are unpaid and limited to close friends or family.
- **Professional:** default for all other work, including work not explicitly classified. Treat it as paid/commercial unless an exception is approved.
Never infer Personal classification from the beneficiary or subject matter.

## Operating principles
1. Evidence over assumption.
2. Security and privacy by design.
3. Explicit ownership and approval for consequential decisions.
4. Reuse where it helps; avoid unnecessary coupling.
5. Incremental delivery and maintainable architecture.
6. Zero ongoing cost is the default until the owner approves otherwise.
7. Human approval for high-impact, risky, financial, or external actions.
8. No silent scope expansion.
9. Accessibility, usability, quality, and operational readiness matter.

## Operating layers and roles
Organization sets direction, governance, policy, portfolio priorities, and risk appetite. Portfolio coordinates cross-project priorities and dependencies. Project teams own scope, architecture, delivery, quality, and acceptance. Engineering/platform owns shared practices and assets. Delivery/operations owns releases, monitoring, support, incidents, and recovery.

Role categories may include Organization Owner, Portfolio Coordinator, Project/Product Owner, Technical/Architecture Lead, Engineering Lead, Security/Privacy Lead, Data Owner, QA/Release Lead, Platform/Operations Lead, and Business/Acceptance Owner. These are role categories, not confirmed assignments. Named role holders and approval limits require owner confirmation.

## Legal and commercial boundaries
Legal entity, registration, jurisdiction, company structure, contracting, invoicing, tax, commercial terms, and external service commitments are not established in this baseline. Do not make commitments on behalf of ASAA; refer these matters to the owner and qualified professionals.
