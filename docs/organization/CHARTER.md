# ASAA Organization Charter

**Version:** 1.0 — initial baseline  
**As of:** 2026-11-14  
**Approval status:** Organizational planning baseline; owner confirmation of open items is pending.

## 1. Identity

ASAA is the umbrella organization and technology ecosystem under which the ASAA organization platform, Portfolio, JARVIS, Templates, and future internal or client-facing products and services are organized.

**Organization identity:** ASAA  
**GitHub organization:** [ASAA1229](https://github.com/ASAA1229)

Do not use a former organization or company name in new plans unless the owner explicitly reintroduces it.

## 2. Purpose

ASAA exists to build, operate, govern, and continuously improve useful software products and technology services. The ecosystem is intended to combine:

1. A coherent organization and governance model.
2. Reusable engineering capabilities, standards, and templates.
3. AI-assisted development, automation, and operational support.
4. Clear product ownership and project accountability.
5. A professional delivery model for internal products and, when authorized, external client work.
6. Sustainable growth while controlling cost, risk, security, and complexity.

## 3. Vision

Build an adaptable technology organization where projects can be planned, delivered, monitored, reused, secured, and improved through consistent governance and shared capabilities—without forcing every product into an identical architecture or release cycle.

## 4. Mission

- Deliver reliable software and digital experiences.
- Reduce repeated engineering effort through reusable assets and templates.
- Use AI and automation with privacy, security, and human approval safeguards.
- Make project status, decisions, risks, requirements, and evidence visible.
- Maintain clear boundaries between planning, implementation, testing, acceptance, and release.
- Prefer incremental delivery and cost-aware choices.

## 5. Organization and project classification

ASAA uses two conceptual project categories:

- **Personal:** only when the owner explicitly labels the project personal. These projects are unpaid and limited to close friends or family.
- **Professional:** the default for all other work, including work not explicitly classified as personal. Professional work is treated as paid/commercial unless a specific exception is approved.

Classification must be recorded for each project or engagement. Do not infer that a project is personal from its subject matter or beneficiary.

## 6. Ecosystem portfolio

- **ASAA:** organization governance and an enterprise project integration platform concept, alongside a broader organizational operations vision that still requires product-boundary confirmation.
- **Portfolio:** multi-profile portfolio and presentation product.
- **JARVIS:** personal AI assistant, developer assistant, and automation product with a longer-term personal AI operating-system direction.
- **Templates:** internal platform for reusable software templates, development assets, and related workflows.

These projects have distinct product boundaries and repositories. Shared policies do not imply shared databases, deployment environments, or unrestricted data access.

## 7. Values and operating principles

- Trust, accountability, and transparency
- Security and privacy by design
- Evidence over assumption
- Explicit ownership and approvals
- Reuse without unnecessary coupling
- Incremental delivery and maintainability
- Cost awareness; zero ongoing cost is the default constraint unless the owner changes it
- Human oversight for consequential or risky automation
- Accessibility, usability, and operational readiness
- No silent scope expansion

## 8. Scope boundaries

This charter describes the organization and its intended ecosystem. It does not itself authorize:
- software implementation or repository changes;
- production deployment or release;
- paid subscriptions, infrastructure, or third-party services;
- collection or sharing of personal/client data;
- financial transactions;
- merging pull requests.

Those actions require the applicable project or organization approval.

## 9. Charter items requiring owner confirmation

- Legal entity, jurisdiction, registration status, and formal company structure
- Final organization chart and named role holders
- Whether the broader HR, payroll, finance, procurement, vendor, and customer-operations vision belongs in the ASAA software product or remains an organizational capability map
- Commercial terms, contracting, invoicing, tax, and client acceptance policy
- Formal mission/vision wording for external publication
