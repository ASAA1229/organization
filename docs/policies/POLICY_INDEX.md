# ASAA Policy Index

This index lists organization-wide policies. A policy marked **Baseline** is an established direction in the current planning context; a policy marked **Proposed** needs owner ratification or operational configuration; **Verification required** means the written rule exists but enforcement has not been proven.

| Policy | Status | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| Branching and change control | Baseline; enforcement verification required | All routine work targets `new`; protect `dev` and `main` |
| Personal vs Professional classification | Baseline | Personal only when explicitly declared; otherwise Professional |
| Cost and procurement | Baseline | Zero-cost default; explicit approval before paid commitment |
| Security and privacy | Baseline principles; controls need project evidence | Least privilege, data minimization, secure defaults, auditability |
| AI and automation safety | Baseline principles; detailed risk tiers need approval | Human approval for consequential/high-risk actions |
| Requirements and change governance | Baseline | Traceability, impact analysis, explicit approval |
| Documentation and reporting | Baseline | Evidence-based status, named owner, review date |
| Data governance | Proposed detailed standard | Classification, ownership, retention, deletion, sharing |
| Incident and continuity management | Proposed detailed standard | Incident severity, response, backup, recovery, lessons learned |
| People and workforce | Proposed; legal review required | Roles, access lifecycle, conduct, confidentiality |
| Client and commercial delivery | Proposed; legal/finance review required | Contract, scope, payment, acceptance, support |
| Supplier and procurement | Proposed | Due diligence, approval, access, renewal and exit |

## Policy lifecycle

Draft → Review → Approved → Effective → Periodic review → Revised or Retired.

Every policy must identify its owner, approver, effective date, scope, exceptions, review interval, and evidence of enforcement. Do not claim a policy is technically enforced merely because it is documented.
