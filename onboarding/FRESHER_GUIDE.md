# Freshers Guide

Welcome to **ASAA1229**! This guide walks a new developer from repository access to their first contribution.

1. **Get access** – Clone `organization` and the six product repos.
2. **Read the Organization Overview** (`ORGANIZATION_OVERVIEW.md`).
3. **Pick a project** – see `projects/README.md` for a short description of each.
4. **Setup your environment** – follow the `onboarding/DEVELOPER_SETUP.md` guide.
5. **Make your first PR** – see `onboarding/CONTRIBUTION_GUIDE.md`.

All commands are shown for PowerShell on Windows.
